Client-audit variants

Five designs for the same audit-services offer. Same content, different structure and tone. Open each one, then compare.

the five

01

Terminal heritage

Evolves the existing terminal aesthetic, retargeted at buyers. A fake shell session tells the engagement story. Credibility through form — "it already looks like us."

04

Engagement timeline

The whole page is the four-step process, running down a spine. Services and guarantees attach to the stage they belong to. Answers "what happens, and when do I get the report."

open → 04-timeline/index.html
05

Decision memo

A narrow single column that reads like an internal memo. Conclusion first, no ornament, everything in descending order of importance. For the reader with 60 seconds.

open → 05-onepager/index.html

how to choose

All five carry identical substance — the same five service lines, the same four-step engagement, the same working terms. Only the structure and tone change, so you can pick on feel rather than on content.

If your buyer is technical — 01 or 05.
If your buyer is procurement or compliance — 02 or 03.
If your buyer is anxious about process — 04.
If you can only ship one and can't decide — 02 is the default that offends nobody.
