How it runs

The audit engagement

The same four steps every time. You always know what happens next and what you are going to receive.

  1. 1

    Scoping

    Agree targets, rules of engagement, and out-of-scope systems in writing before any testing begins.

    Output · written scope & rules of engagement

  2. 2

    Testing

    Manual assessment against the agreed scope, reported as it progresses so findings are never a surprise at the end.

    Output · interim findings as they are confirmed

  3. 3

    Reporting

    A written report: each finding with severity, reproduction steps, business impact, and a concrete remediation.

    Output · the audit report

  4. 4

    Retest

    After remediation, findings are re-tested and the report is updated. Free of charge and without a time limit.

    Output · updated report · no charge, no deadline

Disclosure & working terms

How we work

Terms that hold whether you are a customer or a vendor on the other side of a finding.

    Encrypted communications accepted (PGP on request).

  • No premature publication

    Zero exploit code published prior to a fix.

    Zero client names published without written permission.

  • Credit where it is due

    Medium-severity issues: courtesy credit / hall of fame.
