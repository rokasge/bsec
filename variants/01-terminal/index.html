services

  • 01

    Web Application & API Penetration Testing

    Manual testing to find exploitable flaws before attackers do. Authentication, access control, injection, business-logic abuse, and API authorization.

  • 02

the engagement

How an audit runs, from first email to closed retest.

baltsec@audit:~$ ./engagement --status [1/4]scope ... agreed in writing [2/4]test .... reported live [3/4]report .. impact + remediation [4/4]retest .. free, no time limit report.md written — 0 critical open

  1. Scoping

    Agree targets, rules of engagement, and out-of-scope systems in writing before any testing begins.

  2. Testing

    Manual assessment against the agreed scope, reported as it progresses so findings are never a surprise at the end.

  3. Reporting

    A written report: each finding with severity, reproduction steps, business impact, and a concrete remediation.

  4. Retest

    After remediation, findings are re-tested and the report is updated. Free of charge and without a time limit.

Free retest. Remediate at your own pace — re-testing the fixed findings costs nothing, whenever you are ready.
